A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you join.

Is the work remote?+

Yes. The team works through an online workspace with personal assignments, shared research rooms, and team updates.

How much is the monthly pay?+

The fixed monthly pay is $13 USD per worker. Payment is arranged separately by the team owner. Confirm your work and payment arrangements with the owner.

Do I automatically earn a bug bounty?+

No. A program decides whether a report is eligible and whether a reward is paid. Follow the current scope, reporting requirements, and program rules.

Where do existing members sign in?+

Go to the team sign-in page. Your existing username and password continue to work.

What if the team places are full?+

You can join the CV waitlist for the owner to review. A waitlist submission does not guarantee a role.
